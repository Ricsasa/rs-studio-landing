# RS Studio – Hero copy update specification

## Objective

Replace the current hero copy with messaging that clearly positions RS Studio as a software and digital product company while communicating the company’s long-term mission of supporting local businesses, community-driven organizations, and impact-focused projects.

The primary goal is to ensure visitors immediately understand **what RS Studio builds** (websites, digital products, custom software, and automation systems) and **who it builds for** (small businesses, local entrepreneurs, and organizations creating local value).

The messaging should feel professional, technical, and business-oriented, not charitable or nonprofit.

---

## Hero copy

### Headline

# Software that strengthens local businesses and communities

### Supporting paragraph

RS Studio builds websites, digital products, automation systems, and custom software that help small businesses grow, operate more efficiently, and reach more customers.

We believe great technology should not be reserved for large companies. Our mission is to build practical digital solutions that strengthen local economies, support community-driven projects, and give independent businesses access to the same quality of tools used by larger organizations.

### Closing statement

*When local businesses grow, everyone grows.*

---

## Messaging principles

- Lead with **software and digital products**.
- Position the mission as a **business philosophy**, not a social program.
- Emphasize **growth, efficiency, and competitiveness**.
- Avoid language that sounds like a nonprofit organization or charitable initiative.
- Keep the tone confident, modern, and technically credible.
- The closing statement should remain short, memorable, and emotionally resonant.

---

## Brand positioning

RS Studio is a technology company that believes software can strengthen local businesses, create economic opportunity, and help communities thrive through better digital infrastructure.

The hero should communicate both **technical capability** and **purpose-driven entrepreneurship** within the first screen of the website.

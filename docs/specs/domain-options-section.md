# New Section: Domain Options
## Landing Pages Page — Addition to Implementation Spec

**Position:** After "What We Build" section and before "Build Trust" section
**Purpose:** Explain domain ownership options without overwhelming. Show that both options are professional and viable.
**Note on pricing:** Copy describes options, but pricing ($550 MXN for custom domain, free for .rslink.mx) will be mentioned in FAQ or pricing later, not in this section.

---

## Section Concept

Many service businesses worry about owning their online presence. This section shows that we offer flexible domain options, neither of which locks them into a dependency on RS Studio.

---

## Constraints

- No pricing numbers in the section copy (keep it in FAQ or pricing page later)
- Emphasize choice and ownership, not lock-in
- Keep example domains concrete and realistic (service business examples)
- No emdashes
- Clear comparison without making custom domain seem "better"
- Both options equally professional

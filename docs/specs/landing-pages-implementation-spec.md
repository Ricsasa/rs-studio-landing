## Overview

Reposition the landing pages section from generic "conversion-focused landing page" messaging toward service business positioning. The focus shifts from selling the format (landing page) to selling the business outcome (trust and qualified leads).

**Scope:** Update both English and Spanish versions of this page with new positioning, sections, and copy.

**Key constraint:** Replace all generic AI-language with concrete, service-business-specific copy. No emdashes. Clear, direct communication. Maintain natural tone and idiom in both languages.

### 9. FAQ Section
 
#### Heading
**Current:** `Preguntas frecuentes`
**New:** `Frequently Asked Questions`
 
#### Q1: How do I build trust online before the first conversation?
**New:**
```
Your website does most of this work for you. Show real testimonials from past clients, explain your experience, share case studies or examples of your work, and be clear about your process. Clients decide in seconds if they trust you. A professional website that answers their questions makes that decision easy.
```
 
#### Q2: What should I include on my website?
**New:**
```
At minimum: what you do, who you serve, why clients should trust you (testimonials, experience, results), how to reach you, and a clear next step. Some businesses also benefit from pricing, FAQs, or a booking system. We recommend what makes sense for your business model.
```
 
#### Q3: Can I show my pricing or packages?
**New:**
```
Yes. Many service businesses prefer to show pricing or ranges upfront to filter for the right clients before the first conversation. This saves everyone time. We define this together based on your business model.
```
 
#### Q4: How do people find my website?
**New:**
```
We build your site with basic SEO so it has a foundation. But getting found is an ongoing effort that usually involves: Google Business Profile (especially for local services), appearing in directories your clients use, referrals, and sometimes ads. We can discuss what makes sense for your type of business.
```
 
#### Q5: What happens after someone contacts me through the website?
**New:**
```
That depends on your process. We integrate WhatsApp, email, contact forms, or booking systems depending on what works for you. The goal is to make it as easy as possible for someone to reach you. We also track these conversions so you know the site is working.
```
 
#### Q6: Can I update the content myself?
**New:**
```
Yes. We build your site so you can update text and images directly. We will show you how, or we can handle updates for you on a retainer basis if you prefer.
```

## Constraints & Rules

1. **No emdashes.** Use periods, semicolons, colons, or parentheses instead.
2. **Concrete language only.** Never use: "increase your online presence," "professional digital experience," "modern and optimized," "high-converting design."
3. **Replace with specific copy:** 
   - Instead of "increase your presence": "Help potential clients understand what you do"
   - Instead of "conversion-focused": "Clear service descriptions, testimonials, and contact options"
   - Instead of "professional experience": "A website that explains your services better than a brochure"
4. **Keep "landing page" for SEO value** but make sure it does not dominate the H1 or messaging.
5. **Mention pricing/packages as a feature** in the "What We Build" section, not as a separate offer.
6. **Service business language throughout:** services, trust, credibility, leads, portfolio, testimonials, experience, results, professional process.
